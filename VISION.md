# MediaLooped Vision

## Mission

Turn every family's photos and videos into timeless cinematic memories.

## Vision

A parent should be able to drop an entire vacation folder into MediaLooped and receive a beautiful Netflix-style documentary with almost no effort.

## Core principles

1. **Family first**  
   Technology exists to preserve memories.

2. **Minimal effort**  
   Every feature should reduce manual work.

3. **Story over effects**  
   Emotion matters more than flashy transitions.

4. **Cinematic output**  
   Every finished film should feel calm, premium, and timeless.

5. **Privacy by default**  
   Media stays on the user's computer whenever possible.

6. **Human approval**  
   The family always has final control over what is included.

## Success

When someone watches the film twenty years later, they should remember the journey—not the editing.
