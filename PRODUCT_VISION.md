PRODUCT VISION
# MediaLooped

## Mission

Igniting happy memories and putting a smile back on people's faces.

---

## Vision

MediaLooped doesn't organize photos.

It understands memories.

---

## Founding Principles

1. Memories over media.

2. Emotion before technology.

3. AI enhances memories, never replaces them.

4. Privacy is a feature.

5. Quality over speed.

6. Every interaction should bring joy.

7. AI tells the truth.

8. Help people rediscover forgotten moments.

9. Preserve stories, not files.

---

## Success

A user should smile while reliving a memory.

If that happens, we succeeded.
