# Founding Story

MediaLooped began with one real problem:

A busy father had thousands of meaningful family memories but almost no time to turn them into movies.

The first test library was a curated Banff family trip containing:

- 149 files
- 114 photos
- 35 videos
- 7.48 GB of media

The original goal was simple:

> Drop memories in. Get a movie out.

MediaLooped was founded around three priorities:

1. Family comes first.
2. The user should not need to become a video editor.
3. The finished film should still matter decades later.

The first working prototypes were created on August 6, 2026.
